# Adventure-Works-sales
Project Title: AdventureWorks Sales Analytics & Business Intelligence

Project Description:

Analyzed sales, customer, and product data using SQL, Microsoft Excel, and Power BI to evaluate business performance, identify sales trends, and generate actionable business insights.

Key Responsibilities & Contributions:

* Analyzed sales data to evaluate revenue performance across products, customers, and geographical regions.
* Used SQL queries, joins, aggregations, subqueries, and filtering to extract and analyze business data.
* Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to monitor sales performance, revenue trends, product performance, and regional sales.
* Created an Excel dashboard using PivotTables, charts, and interactive filters to summarize key sales metrics.
* Analyzed product-wise and region-wise sales performance to identify high-performing and underperforming categories.
* Evaluated customer purchasing patterns and sales trends to support data-driven business decisions.
* Tracked key performance indicators (KPIs) to summarize sales performance and support business reporting.
* Transformed raw data into meaningful visualizations and reports to communicate business insights effectively.

Tools & Technologies: SQL, Microsoft Excel, Power BI, DAX, Data Cleaning, Data Visualization, Sales Analytics, Business Intelligence, KPI Reporting.

Project Outcome: Developed sales dashboards and SQL-based analysis to improve visibility into business performance, customer trends, and product sales.
